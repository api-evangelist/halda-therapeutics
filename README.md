# Halda Therapeutics

Halda Therapeutics was a clinical-stage biotechnology company and Yale University spinout developing oral, targeted cancer therapies on its proprietary RIPTAC (Regulated Induced Proximity TArgeting Chimera) "hold and kill" platform. Its lead program, HLD-0915, is a first-in-class oral RIPTAC therapeutic for metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC), alongside earlier candidates for breast, lung, and other tumor types.

Backed by investors including Canaan Partners, Halda was acquired by Johnson & Johnson in 2025 for approximately $3.05 billion in cash, and its platform was folded into Johnson & Johnson Innovative Medicine's oncology pipeline. The former haldatx.com now redirects to jnj.com/innovativemedicine.

As a therapeutics developer, Halda operates no public API, developer portal, documentation, or SDK surface. This repository is maintained in the API Evangelist network as a company identity and portfolio-graph record (surfaced via Canaan Partners), not as an API producer.
